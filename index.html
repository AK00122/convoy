THE CONVOY STORY

A journey of exceptional things.

CONVOY takes its name from the caravan — a journey across places, cultures and crafts. We seek exceptional craftsmanship and bring it to people who value the story behind what they own.

For our handmade rugs, provenance matters. We aim to document the making journey — from design and loom to the hands of the artisan and the finished rug.
